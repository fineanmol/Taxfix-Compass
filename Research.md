From what I can tell, **Quanto: Expense Tracker** is a relatively new, independently developed personal finance app focused on **manual expense tracking** rather than bank account syncing. Its main selling point is simplicity, speed, and privacy. ([App Store][1])

Here's what it offers:

### Core features

* **Quick expense and income logging** with a minimal interface.
* **Budgets** to help monitor spending.
* **Analytics** with weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly summaries.
* **Charts** that show spending trends over time.
* **Custom categories** with no practical limit.
* **Multiple accounts** (e.g., cash, checking, savings).
* **Multi-currency support**, useful for travel or international users.
* **Recurring transactions** for bills or regular income.
* **CSV import/export** for moving your data in or out.
* **No account required** to start using the app. ([App Store][1])

### Recent improvements

The developer has been shipping updates frequently. Recent additions include:

* An **Overview dashboard** with more detailed analytics.
* Better recurring transaction management, including reminders.
* More sorting and filtering options.
* Transfers between accounts.
* Custom month start dates (useful if your budget follows your payday instead of the calendar month).
* Transaction shortcuts for faster entry.
* Additional currencies and various performance improvements. ([App Store][2])

### Privacy

The app advertises itself as **privacy-first**:

* Your financial records stay on your device.
* You don't need to create an account.
* There is **no bank account integration**, which some people prefer for privacy. ([App Store][1])

One caveat is that the App Store privacy information indicates the app may use **device identifiers for advertising and marketing purposes**, even though transaction data is intended to remain local. It's worth reviewing the developer's privacy policy if this is important to you. ([App Store][1])

### Pricing

The app is free to download, with an optional **Premium** subscription that unlocks features such as:

* Multiple accounts
* Recurring transactions
* Data export

Pricing varies by region, but in the US it's approximately:

* $2.99/month
* $24.99/year
* $59.99 lifetime purchase ([App Store][1])

### Who it's best for

Quanto is a good fit if you:

* Prefer **manual budgeting** instead of automatic bank syncing.
* Want a **clean, minimalist** interface.
* Care about keeping your financial data local.
* Don't need investment tracking, credit score monitoring, or complex financial planning.

It may not be the best choice if you're looking for:

* Automatic bank transaction imports.
* Household or family budgeting with shared accounts.
* Net worth tracking or investment management.
* Advanced reporting comparable to tools like YNAB or Monarch Money.

Overall, it appears to be aimed at people who simply want to **record expenses quickly and understand where their money goes**, without the complexity of full-featured financial software.
